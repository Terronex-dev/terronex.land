Privacy

A short note on what the public terronex.land estimate form collects and where it goes.

This page is informative, not legal counsel. Jason will review it later as the practice grows.

What the estimate form sends

The Request estimate form collects name, company, email, phone (optional), and project details (location, duration, parcel count, line length or area, services, and notes). Submit opens your email client with a message to hello@terronex.land. The site does not store that form on a Terronex server; the draft lives in your mail client until you send it.

We use what you send only to respond to the estimate request and related project follow-up. We do not sell it.

If the email client does not open, write hello@terronex.land directly.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use a cookie banner and does not use advertising cookies. Pages may load Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-oriented page-view beacon. It is not ad targeting.
